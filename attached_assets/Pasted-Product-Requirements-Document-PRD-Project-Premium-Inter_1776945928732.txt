Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Project: Premium Interactive 3D Greeting Card Generator

1. Project Overview

We are building a premium, mobile-responsive, interactive web-based greeting card application. It features a physics-based 3D unboxing sequence, circular interactive UI elements, high-performance particle explosion effects, and a cinematic final message reveal.

The app must have two primary sides:

The Configurator: A simple form where a user selects an Occasion, a Relationship, inputs a Name, and optionally edits the final message. It generates a unique shareable link (e.g., /?to=Rahul&template=thank_you_friend).

The Experience: The final interactive card that the recipient views.

2. The Recipient Experience (Animation Sequence)

The core experience is strictly divided into four distinct phases. Note: Smooth transitions between these phases are critical.

Phase 1: The Hook & The Envelope

Visuals: * A pulsating, golden "✨ A Surprise For You! ✨" text sits at the top of the screen to build anticipation.

A luxurious, 3D CSS Golden envelope sits in the center of the screen against a dark, ambient radial-gradient background.

Interaction: A "Slide to unlock" drag-slider sits at the bottom. The user must physically drag the slider to the right to break the seal.

Phase 2: The Unboxing & The Orbs (No Central Card)

Action: Upon unlocking, the slider and "Surprise" text fade out. The envelope's wax seal disappears, the flap rotates open (3D X-axis), and the entire envelope drops down off-screen and fades out.

The Circle Math: Simultaneously, 4 to 6 "Hobby/Trait Orbs" (containing emojis) spawn and animate into a tight, perfect circle in the center of the screen.

Implementation Note: Use trigonometry (x = centerX + radius * cos(angle)) to dynamically position the orbs based on screen size.

Progress UI: A simple text prompt appears at the bottom: "Tap the orbs! (0 / X)".

Phase 3: The Interaction

Action: The recipient clicks the floating orbs.

Effects on Click: 1. The clicked orb gets marked (e.g., grayscaled/lowered opacity) and stops floating.
2. Particle Burst: A mini-burst of that orb's specific emoji explodes from the exact X/Y coordinate of the clicked orb using HTML5 Canvas.
3. Centered Tooltip: A stylized tooltip containing a personalized compliment pops up in the exact center of the screen.
4. Z-Index Layering: The tooltip must have a lower z-index than the orbs, so the floating orbs frame the edges of the message card without being cut off.

Phase 4: The Grand Finale

Trigger: Once all orbs have been clicked, wait ~1.5 seconds.

The Blow Up: The circular orbs dramatically explode outward, flying completely off the screen radially.

The Final Card: A beautiful, glassmorphism "Final Card" scales and fades into the center of the screen. It displays a title prefix, the Recipient's Name, a bouncy "🎉" emoji, and the final personalized message. (Do not include cheesy "Grand Reveal" headers).

Confetti: A massive screen-wide gold, silver, and white confetti burst fires from the center and rains down from the top.

3. Technical Specifications

Framework: React (Next.js highly recommended for URL parameter parsing and SEO).

Styling: Tailwind CSS.

Responsiveness: Must be 100% mobile-friendly. Use CSS min() functions (e.g., width: min(320px, 85vw)) to ensure the envelope, cards, and slider scale perfectly on small screens like iPhone SE without overflowing.

Animations: Framer Motion (or GSAP). Crucial for handling the trigonometry of placing orbs in a circle, stagger effects, and the final explosion sequence.

Canvas Engine: Native HTML5 Canvas for the high-performance particle engine (handling both elongated metallic confetti and emoji bursts).
