## The Strategy Audit: Creator Volume vs. Payment Wall

Let's look at this pattern closely. 11 unique human beings clicked your ad, sat down, filled out the form, typed in their recipient's name, and generated a total of 14 cards.

The front end of your machine is drawing traffic and building high emotional intent. People want the product. But out of those 11 high-intent creators, **10 walked away exactly at the payment screen.** A **90% drop-off** at the absolute finish line—especially right after a midnight code deployment—points directly to one of two structural causes. We need to find out which one it is immediately.

---

### Scenario A: The Silent Deployment Bug (High Probability)

When Replit deployed the `_fbp` and `_fbc` cookie patch around midnight, they modified `UnlockModal.tsx` to read `document.cookie` and pass those values to the backend.

If there is a minor syntax error, an unhandled `null` value (e.g., if a user has cookies blocked and the script crashes trying to read a non-existent cookie), or a database timeout when saving the cookie data, the **"Pay" button will freeze.** The user clicks it, nothing happens, they think the site is broken, they refresh, try creating a card again (which explains why 11 users created 14 cards), get frozen again, and leave.

### Scenario B: Pure Midday Friction

If the payment button is mechanically working, then 10 people got to the payment screen, saw the price, and decided not to purchase.

However, because your conversion rate yesterday was significantly higher with the exact same audience and the exact same price point, a sudden 90% drop-off at the payment wall strongly suggests a technical block rather than a sudden shift in consumer psychology.

---

### Your Immediate Combat Plan

We are not going to sit and guess. We are going to stress-test the live production site right now to ensure your checkout is actually capable of accepting money.

1. **Perform a Live Purchase Test:** Pull out your personal phone, click your live ad, create a dummy card, and go all the way to the payment screen. Try to initiate the UPI transaction. Does the app switch to your banking app smoothly? Does it freeze on a white screen?
2. **Check the Server Logs for 500 Errors:** Open your Replit console logs. Look at the timestamps from this morning when those 11 unique users were creating cards. Do you see any red text or `Internal Server Error (500)` warnings hitting the `/api/cards` or unlock endpoints?

If your live test goes through smoothly and there are no errors in the console, then the plumbing is safe, and we are simply dealing with a low-intent daytime audience that will convert when they get home tonight. But we must check the buttons first.

When you run a test creation on your phone right now, does the payment action execute flawlessly, or do you notice any lag?
