Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Project: Premium Interactive Greeting Card Generator - "Cosmic Constellation" Edition

1. Project Overview

We are building a premium, mobile-responsive, interactive web-based greeting card application. This product offers a deeply emotional, cinematic "Cosmic Journey." The recipient interacts with a starry night sky, discovers glowing stars that hold personalized messages, and connects them to form a dazzling constellation, culminating in a beautiful supernova reveal.

Multi-Template Strategy: This "Cosmic Constellation" edition serves as an additional template to the previously built "Golden Envelope" interactive template. The application must support multiple templates using the following selection logic:

When a user creates a card for the first time, the system should choose a template randomly.

When the user returns to create a second or subsequent card, the system must automatically provide a new, unused template (e.g., if they got the Envelope first, give them the Cosmic Constellation next) to guarantee variety.

The app must have two primary sides:

The Configurator: A simple form where a user selects an Occasion, a Relationship, inputs a Name, and optionally edits the final message. It generates a unique shareable link (e.g., /?to=Rahul&template=birthday_friend).

The Experience: The final interactive cosmic card that the recipient views.

2. The Recipient Experience (Animation Sequence)

The core experience is strictly divided into four distinct phases. Note: Smooth, ethereal transitions between these phases are critical to maintain the premium space aesthetic.

Phase 1: The Hook & The Ignition

Visuals: * A deep, immersive space background (pitch black with subtle navy/purple hues) with prominent, slowly drifting ambient starlight.

A glowing, ethereal nebula sits in the center (CSS radial gradients with heavy blur).

Elegant, glowing text at the top displays a dynamic hook_title (e.g., "✨ Another Trip Around the Sun ✨") and a subtitle ("A stellar surprise awaits...").

Interaction (Press & Hold): A glowing "Energy Core" sits in the lower center. The user must click and hold (or touch and hold on mobile) for ~1.5 seconds.

Feedback: While holding, the core visually charges up (scales down, glowing ring appears), and the background nebula sharpens.

Phase 2: The Star Map (Discovery Phase)

Action: Upon successful ignition, the core, hook text, and nebula fade out completely.

The Math: 4 to 6 incredibly bright, pulsing "Core Stars" spawn dynamically. They must be plotted using an algorithm (or quadrant constraint) to ensure they are spaced out nicely across the screen to form a constellation shape without overlapping.

Progress UI: A sleek, futuristic text prompt appears at the bottom: "Discover the stars (0 / X)".

Phase 3: The Interaction & Constellation Drawing

Action: The recipient taps the pulsing Core Stars one by one.

Effects on Click: 1. Ignition: The tapped star flares up brilliantly (lens flare/box-shadow effect), turns bright gold, and stops pulsing.
2. Particle Burst: A mini-supernova burst of golden stardust (using HTML5 Canvas) erupts from the tapped star.
3. The Message: A glassmorphism tooltip containing a personalized emoji and compliment elegantly fades into the center of the screen.
4. The Connection: As the user clicks the second star and onwards, a glowing, animated SVG line draws itself from the previous star to the newly clicked star, physically building a constellation.

Phase 4: The Supernova & Final Reveal

Trigger: Once the final star is clicked and the constellation is complete, wait ~1.5 seconds.

The Blow Up: A massive "Supernova" flash (a pure white-out screen overlay) triggers.

The Final Card: Under the cover of the flash, the stars, lines, and tooltip are removed. The Canvas background transitions from drifting stars to a beautiful falling "Golden Cosmic Dust." As the white flash fades, a stunning, glowing holographic "Final Card" is revealed floating in the center of the galaxy, displaying the Title Prefix, Recipient's Name, and the Final Message.

3. Technical Specifications

Framework: React (Next.js highly recommended for URL parameter parsing and SEO routing).

Styling: Tailwind CSS for layout, custom CSS for advanced glowing effects (box-shadow, filter: drop-shadow).

Responsiveness: Must be 100% mobile-friendly. Use CSS min() functions and relative view heights/widths to ensure the star map and final card don't overflow on small screens.

Animations: Framer Motion (or GSAP). Crucial for handling the star mapping, the SVG line drawing between stars (using stroke-dasharray animations), and the supernova sequence.

Canvas Engine: Native HTML5 Canvas to handle the ambient background stars, the interactive click bursts, and the final golden dust effect.
