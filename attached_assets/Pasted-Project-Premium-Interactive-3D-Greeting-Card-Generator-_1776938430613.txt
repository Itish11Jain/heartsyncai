Project: Premium Interactive 3D Greeting Card Generator

1. Project Overview

We are building a premium, interactive web-based greeting card application. It features a physics-based 3D unboxing sequence, circular interactive UI elements, particle explosion effects, and a cinematic final message reveal.

The app must have two sides:

The Configurator: A simple form where a user selects an Occasion, a Relationship, inputs a Name, and optionally edits the final message. It generates a unique link.

The Experience: The final interactive card that the recipient views.

2. The Recipient Experience (Animation Sequence)

The core experience is divided into four distinct phases.

Phase 1: The Sealed Envelope

Visual: A dark, luxurious 3D CSS envelope sits in the center of the screen.

Interaction: A "Slide to Open" drag-slider sits at the bottom.

Action: The user must physically drag the slider to the right to break the seal.

Phase 2: The Core Reveal & The Circle

Action: Upon unlocking, the envelope flap rotates open (3D X-axis). The central card slides up and scales up.

The Circle Math: 4 to 6 "Hobby/Trait Orbs" spawn and animate into a perfect circle around the central card.

Implementation Note: Use trigonometry (x = centerX + radius * cos(angle), y = centerY + radius * sin(angle)) to dynamically position the orbs based on screen size.

Phase 3: The Interaction

Action: The recipient clicks the orbs in the circle.

Effect: Clicking an orb reveals a stylized tooltip containing a generic or personalized compliment.

Particle Effect: A mini-burst of the orb's specific emoji explodes from the exact X/Y coordinate of the clicked orb.

State Tracking: The system tracks how many unique orbs have been clicked.

Phase 4: The Grand Finale

Trigger: Once all orbs have been clicked (or a "Reveal Message" button appears and is clicked).

The Blow Up: The circular orbs dramatically explode outward off the screen using a stagger animation.

The Final Message: The central card flips 180 degrees (or fades into a glowing state), and the "Final Message" typographically animates onto the screen with a premium, glowing gold-foil effect, accompanied by a massive screen-wide confetti burst.

4. Technical Stack Requirements

Framework: React (Next.js recommended for handling dynamic routing/shareable links, e.g., /?to=Rahul&template=thank_you_friend).

Styling: Tailwind CSS.

Animations: Framer Motion or GSAP (Crucial for the circular math, stagger effects, and the final explosion sequence).

Canvas: HTML5 Canvas for the high-performance particle engine (confetti and emoji bursts).
