Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Project: Premium Interactive Greeting Card Generator - "Retro Vinyl Mixtape" Edition

1. Project Overview

We are building a premium, mobile-responsive, interactive web-based greeting card application. This iteration offers a warm, high-contrast "Vintage Audiophile" experience. The recipient interacts with a luxurious ivory-and-copper record player, discovers floating musical notes that reveal personalized messages, and unlocks a crisp, minimalist "Platinum Edition" record sleeve.

Aesthetic Note (CRITICAL): Do NOT use dark mode, pure black, or heavy neon colors. The palette must be highly aesthetic, bright, and feature high contrast: deep vintage beige backgrounds (#D8CBB8 to #F4ECE1), pristine ivory/white components for the player to make it pop, soft charcoal vinyl, deep mocha typography (#4A3C31), and rich copper/gold foil accents.

Multi-Template Strategy: This "Retro Vinyl Mixtape" edition serves as an additional template to the growing library. The application must support multiple templates using the following selection logic:

When a user creates a card for the first time, the system should choose a template randomly.

When the user returns to create a second or subsequent card, the system must automatically provide a new, unused template to guarantee variety.

2. The Recipient Experience (Animation Sequence)

The core experience is divided into four distinct phases.

Phase 1: The Hook & The Play Button

Visuals: * A warm, deep vintage beige radial gradient background with subtle multiply-blend film grain.

A sleek 3D record player sits in the center. The base is a crisp ivory/white to provide high contrast against the beige background. It features charcoal vinyl and a copper tonearm.

Elegant, gold-foil serif text at the top displays a dynamic mixtape_title (e.g., "Vol 1: Another Trip Around the Sun").

Interaction (Tactile Button): A premium, metallic "PRESS TO PLAY" button sits below the player.

Feedback: Clicking the button physically depresses it. The button fades out, and the tonearm automatically rotates smoothly and drops onto the vinyl.

Phase 2: The Tracklist (Discovery Phase)

Action: Upon the tonearm dropping, the record begins to spin steadily. Ambient copper "equalizer waves" radiate outward from the center of the record player using HTML5 Canvas.

The Math: 4 to 6 glowing "Musical Notes" float up around the record and bounce gently in a dynamic pattern.

Progress UI: A sleek mocha text prompt appears at the bottom: "PLAYING TRACKS (0 / X)".

Phase 3: The Groove (Interaction)

Action: The recipient taps the floating musical notes one by one.

Effects on Click: 1. Ignition: The tapped note brightens, scales up, and anchors in place.
2. Particle Burst: A mini-burst of warm copper/peach sparks (using Canvas) erupts from the tapped note.
3. The Message: A white glassmorphism "Cassette Tape" tooltip containing a personalized emoji and compliment fades into the center of the screen.
4. The Connection: The ambient copper equalizer waves in the background grow slightly more intense with each note clicked.

Phase 4: The Platinum Record Sleeve (Final Reveal)

Trigger: Once the final note is clicked, wait ~1.5 seconds.

The Transition: The tonearm lifts off. The record spins at hyper-speed. The entire record player and the notes smoothly slide down and fade off-screen.

The Environment: The background Canvas switches from radiating waves to a prominent, magical shower of large, slow-falling gold, copper, and bright white dust to create a high-contrast sparkling effect.

The Final Card: A crisp, white "Platinum Edition" record sleeve slides up into the center. It displays the dedication (Title Prefix), the Recipient's Name in gold-foil typography, and the Final Message in deep mocha.

3. Technical Specifications

Framework: React (Next.js highly recommended for URL parameter parsing and SEO routing).

Styling: Tailwind CSS for layout. Custom CSS required for box-shadow depth, gradients, and the @keyframes vinyl spin.

Responsiveness: Must be 100% mobile-friendly. Use CSS min() functions (e.g., width: min(340px, 90vw)) so the record player and final album cover scale perfectly on small screens without breaking layout.

Animations: Framer Motion (or GSAP). Crucial for handling the tactile button press, tonearm rotation, floating musical notes, and the synchronized phase transitions.

Canvas Engine: Native HTML5 Canvas to handle the growing equalizer waves, the click-triggered copper sparks, and the final prominent golden/white dust rain.
