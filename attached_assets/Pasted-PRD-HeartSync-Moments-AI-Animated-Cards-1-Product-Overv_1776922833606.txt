PRD: HeartSync Moments (AI Animated Cards)
1. Product Overview
Goal: To provide users with a "social glue" tool that helps them express emotions (Thank You, Sorry, Love) through high-quality, personalized animated cards.
Target Audience: 18–35 years old, Tier 2 & 3 cities.
Core Value: Moves the user from "advice" to "actionable connection" by solving the "what do I send next?" problem.

2. User Stories
The "First Date" Follow-up: "As a user who just finished a coffee date, I want to send a unique 'Thank You' card so I stand out and secure a second date."

The "Oops" Moment: "As someone who made a mistake, I want a creative 'Sorry' card that uses humor to break the tension without being awkward."

The "Relationship Milestone": "As a boyfriend, I want to send an animated 'Thinking of You' card that feels more premium than a basic WhatsApp GIF."

3. Functional Requirements
A. Simple Input Flow (4 Steps)
The system shall provide a frictionless 4-step wizard to gather data:

Name: Recipient's name.

Purpose: Selection from four categories: Thank You, Sorry, I Love You, Feel Good.

Relation: Selection from: Date, Friend, Girlfriend/Boyfriend, Wife/Husband.

Picture (Optional): Upload a photo to be integrated into the animation.

B. AI Generation Engine
Template Library: A library of 50+ unique animation templates ranging from "Minimalist Chic" to "Vibrant Maximalism".

Personalization: The AI shall inject the recipient's name and relationship-specific "Simple English" messages into the animation.

Photo Integration: If a photo is uploaded, the AI will use "Smart Cropping" to place it within the card's design.

C. Output & Sharing
Format: High-quality MP4 or GIF (optimized for WhatsApp and Instagram).

Shareability: One-tap sharing to WhatsApp, Instagram Stories, and direct download.

4. Design & UI/UX (Tier 2/3 Focus)
Language: Use "Easy English" (e.g., "Tell us who it is for" instead of "Identify the recipient").

Visuals: High-contrast buttons and large, legible typography.

Micro-interactions: Use "dopamine delivery" animations during the card generation process to keep the user engaged.

5. Technical Requirements (Replit Stack)
Frontend: React.js or Next.js for a responsive, mobile-first interface.

Backend: Python (FastAPI/Flask) to handle AI image/video processing requests.

AI Model Integration: * Text: LLM (e.g., GPT-4o) to generate the custom message based on "Relation" and "Purpose."

Animation: Integration with a video generation API (e.g., Replicate or Cloudinary) to render the 50+ templates.

Storage: AWS S3 or CloudFront for hosting generated media files.

6. Success Metrics (KPIs)
Feature Adoption: % of HeartSync users who create at least one card per week.

Viral Coefficient: Number of new app signups originating from the "Made with HeartSync AI" watermark on shared cards.

User Satisfaction: Average rating of the "Creativity" of the 50 templates.

7. Strategic Positioning in HeartSync AI
This feature should be marketed as the "Aura Delivery" tool.

Placement: A prominent "Send a Moment" button on the main dashboard.

Upsell: Free users get 3 cards/month; Premium HeartSync members get unlimited access to all 50+ premium templates.
